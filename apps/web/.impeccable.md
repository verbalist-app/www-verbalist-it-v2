## Design Context

### Users
**Mixed B2B audience for an Italian-first SEO content tool**:
- **SMB marketing/SEO managers**: own content + SEO themselves, evaluate tools quickly between meetings, budget-conscious. Need to see ROI fast.
- **Content/SEO teams at mid-market & enterprise** (current customers: Rentokil, Pompea, Meccanotecnica): producing content at scale across multiple markets and languages. Need confidence in the pipeline, not novelty.
- **SEO agencies & freelancers**: scaling output without hiring, multi-client workflows.

Primary context: comparing Verbalist against Clearscope/Surfer/Frase/ChatGPT during a vendor evaluation. Reading on desktop in Italian. Already SEO-literate — they don't need basics explained; they need to see the *workflow* (SERP analysis → brief → generation → optimization) and trust the output.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: confident · technical · restrained.

- **Voice**: matter-of-fact Italian B2B. No marketing fluff ("trasforma keyword, risultati Google e competitor in contenuti SEO strutturati"). States what the tool does and lets the workflow do the persuading.
- **Tone**: editorial-grade clarity over hype. Agent names (Esploratore, Estrattore, Redattore) are evocative without being playful — like a craft workshop, not a toy.
- **Emotional goal**: trust through specificity. The reader should feel "these people understand the problem and have built something rigorous," not "wow, what a fun website."

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual tone**: clean modern SaaS in the Linear/Vercel/Stripe family, but pulled toward the data-dense seriousness of Clearscope/Surfer. Light-only. Big screenshots are central — the product is the dashboard, so let it carry the weight.

**Existing system to respect**:
- Custom OKLCH "mist" palette — cool, blue-tinted neutrals (no pure grays). Keep it.
- Familjen Grotesk (display, distinctive) + Inter (body, industry default but acceptable).
- Light theme only going forward.

**Anti-references** (the user explicitly wants to AVOID these):
- AI-slop SaaS template look: glassmorphism, glow accents, identical feature card grids, hero metric layouts, gradient text on metrics, dark-mode-with-cyan. Note: the AI-slop fingerprint is *saturated dark-mode glow*, not muted light-mode color. Light-mode `Wallpaper` backdrops (desaturated blue/purple/green/brown with noise) are part of the design — they add warmth and are wanted on featured screenshots (hero + key feature blocks). Don't strip them wholesale.
- Generic Tailwind UI / template aesthetics — even though the site is built on the Lexington Flabbergasted template, the goal is to push it past template-default.
- Playful/cute branding — this is a tool for professionals, not consumers.

### Design Principles
1. **Show the work, with a frame that holds it.** The dashboard screenshots are the strongest proof. The light-mode `Wallpaper` backdrop (desaturated gradient + noise) gives them a photographic frame and is part of the brand — keep it on the hero screenshot and the two main feature-block screenshots. Drop the wallpaper on secondary screenshots and on customer/avatar imagery — color should be a deliberate choice, not a uniform default. Avoid stacking outer cards (`bg-mist-950/2.5 p-2`) around a wallpapered screenshot — the wallpaper *is* the frame.
2. **Tinted neutrals, sparse accent.** Stay inside the mist palette. Color is communication — used to draw the eye to one CTA per section, not to decorate. No rainbow icon backgrounds.
3. **Typographic hierarchy does the heavy lifting.** Familjen Grotesk for distinctive H1/H2; size and weight differences carry rhythm. Resist adding decorative elements to compensate for weak hierarchy.
4. **Italian-restrained, not minimal-empty.** Restraint means deliberate density, not whitespace for its own sake. Pricing tables, feature lists, comparison data — show them confidently, full and well-organized.
5. **Earn every interaction.** No bouncy hovers, no glow-on-hover decoration, no scroll-jacking. Motion is reserved for state changes that need acknowledgment (form submit, accordion open). The site should feel fast and direct.
